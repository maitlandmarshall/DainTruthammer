# Dain Requisition Wishlist

## One-Line Summary
An idea-stage requisition list for scrolls, magical curios and laboratory supplies that could teach Dain principles useful for inventing uniquely odd and apparently useless spells.

## Best Scroll Requests From Ben

These avoid spells already recorded in Dain's book, including `Silent Image` and `Rope Trick`. Dain can normally copy only wizard spells of a level he can prepare, so 1st- and 2nd-level wizard scrolls are the cleanest requests at Wizard 4.

### First Priority: Spellmaking Principles
1. **Magic Mouth** (`2nd`, ritual) — **[Acquired: 5 spell scrolls on 2026-08-29]**: programmable triggers, conditional behaviour and a permanent magical effect; perhaps the single best foundation for spells that do one ridiculous thing under extremely specific circumstances. Ownership, carrier and final allocation remain [To verify].
2. **Nystul's Magic Aura** (`2nd`): teaches Dain how magical detection, false signatures and classification can be manipulated—the proper science of making one spell pretend to be another sort of nonsense.
3. **Identify** (`1st`, ritual): less useless, but indispensable laboratory equipment in spell form; it gives Dain a repeatable way to inspect strange items and compare finished experiments. Request the reusable `100 gp` pearl as well.
4. **Illusory Script** (`1st`, ritual): selective readers, deceptive writing and calligrapher synergy; an excellent basis for spells involving absurd footnotes, private instructions or messages that only reveal themselves to the wrong person.
5. **Unseen Servant** (`1st`, ritual): a bounded, obedient conjured force capable of trivial tasks—the ideal ancestor for very narrow helper spells.
6. **Tenser's Floating Disk** (`1st`, ritual): persistent force geometry, following behaviour and load limits; useful for researching platforms that carry almost anything except the thing one actually needs.

### Second Priority: Useful Ingredients For Useless Results
7. **Alarm** (`1st`, ritual): programmable boundaries and triggers; useful for bells that detect highly specific inconveniences rather than intruders.
8. **Enlarge/Reduce** (`2nd`): scaling creatures and objects; excellent for size-based spell experiments and disproportionate solutions.
9. **Continual Flame** (`2nd`): a permanent flame that does not produce normal heat; ideal research for light that fails at the one traditional duty of fire.
10. **Grease** (`1st`): surface alteration, involuntary movement and practical indignity. Dain has used grease in prior play, but it is not listed in his spellbook, so formal acquisition would resolve that gap.
11. **Comprehend Languages** (`1st`, ritual): supports old-scroll research and lets Dain inspect magical writing before deciding it deserves improvement.
12. **Arcane Lock** (`2nd`): conditional access and magical permission rules; excellent raw material for doors that open only under unnecessarily peculiar circumstances.

### Ask If Ben Has Exotic Stock
- **Skywrite** (`2nd`, ritual): enormous temporary cloud writing; spectacularly public and gloriously impractical. [To verify allowed sourcebook]
- **Pyrotechnics** (`2nd`): turns an existing flame into smoke or fireworks; useful for non-damaging spectacle research. [To verify allowed sourcebook]
- A **cantrip tutor** for `Mending` or `Prestidigitation`; under normal rules Dain cannot simply copy a cantrip scroll into his spellbook, so instruction or a table-approved research route would be more useful than buying the scroll blindly.

## Magical Curios To Ask Ben About

Ask first for retired, cosmetically damaged, unfashionable or otherwise unwanted stock; Dain wants principles and peculiarities more than resale value.

1. **Hat of Wizardry**: the strongest thematic request—an arcane focus that may let a wizard attempt an unknown cantrip, directly supporting Dain's appetite for unfamiliar magical principles. [To verify availability and exact table rules]
2. **Wand of Conducting**: nearly pure magical frivolity and therefore exemplary research material.
3. **Pipe of Smoke Monsters**: turns breath and smoke into tiny illusory creatures; a compact lesson in useless conjuration and presentation.
4. **Mystery Key**: an unreliable key that sometimes opens a lock, demonstrating narrow probability magic with no respect for planning.
5. **Cloak of Billowing**: magically excellent at drama and almost nothing else.
6. **Staff of Adornment**: suspends small objects for display; potentially useful for studying stable, trivial levitation.
7. **Alchemy Jug** or **Immovable Rod**: more valuable and genuinely useful, but both invite strange experiments far beyond their obvious function.
8. **Failed scrolls, cracked foci, harmless cursed curios and defective minor items**: request quarantined examples Ben considers safe to study rather than use.

## Quartermaster Scribing And Experiment Kit

### Essential
- Two blank spellbooks: one working book and one waterproofed backup.
- Waterproof scroll cases and a padded, lockable document chest.
- Fine inks and reagents sufficient for several 1st- and 2nd-level transcriptions; under the campaign's apparent 2014 baseline, budget `50 gp per spell level` and `2 hours per spell level`, subject to Tom's ruling.
- Fresh parchment or vellum, spare ink, nibs and replacement calligrapher's supplies.
- A complete component pouch plus labelled glass vials and small specimen bags.

### Components For The Priority Scrolls
- One pearl worth at least `100 gp` and an owl feather for `Identify`.
- Honeycomb and jade dust worth at least `10 gp` for `Magic Mouth`.
- Lead-based ink worth at least `10 gp` for `Illusory Script`.
- A tiny bell and fine silver wire for `Alarm`.
- A small dropper vial of mercury for `Tenser's Floating Disk`.
- Ruby dust worth at least `50 gp` for `Continual Flame`.
- Gold dust worth at least `25 gp` for `Arcane Lock`.

### Experimental Odds And Ends
- Copper and silver wire, bells, chalk, sealing wax, twine, needles, feathers, mirrors, lenses, magnets or lodestones, coloured powders, glass beads and empty vials.
- Scrap leather, cloth, fur, mail links and small metal plates for testing garment and armour enchantments.
- Heatproof gloves, long tongs, a sand bucket, a heavy blanket and a ventilated lockbox for experiments that develop fewer manners than expected.

## Recommended Opening Request

> Ben, I need scrolls that are too narrow, unfashionable or ridiculous for sensible wizards—especially Magic Mouth, Magic Aura, Identify and Illusory Script. If you have failed scrolls or harmless magical curios gathering dust, I would like those too. The quartermasters can supply the books, ink, components and something fireproof to put the mistakes in.
