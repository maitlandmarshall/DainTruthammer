# Dain as Smith Apprentice

## One-Line Summary
A non-canon, idea-stage version of Dain's past where he was expected to become a Truthammer master smith but began forging useless impressions instead.

## Core Idea
- Dain was not always just a wizard.
- He may have been expected to become a Truthammer master smith.
- His father may have been a serious Truthammer smith, master blacksmith, or forge authority working at the volcanic forge in the [Truthhammer Mountains](../../Places/Truthhammer%20Mountains.md).
- Dain may not have been bad at smithing. With strength, intellect, and dwarven background, he may have had real potential.
- The problem is that he learned the wrong things.

## What His Father Wanted Him To Learn
- Hammer rhythm.
- Quenching.
- Volcanic heat control.
- Rune alignment.
- Oath-metal.
- Ancestral forge practice.
- Proper seriousness around sacred dwarven machinery.
- Family craft and the responsibilities of the Truthammer name.

## What Dain Noticed Instead
- The pause before heat becomes color.
- The panic of water becoming steam.
- The embarrassment of a failed blade.
- The silence after correction.
- The way a forge listens.
- The cold wind in the upper vents.
- The emotional pressure of a sacred room.
- The magic in useless or discarded impressions.

## Thematic Read
- His father saw heat, pressure, timing, metal, craft, reliability, and tradition.
- Dain noticed thresholds, residue, almost-meanings, sensory leftovers, and social pressure.
- This makes Dain not a failed smith, but a smith who started forging something no one recognized: a magical reservoir inside himself.

## Truthammer Magic Texture
- Respectable Truthammer magic could be stored in:
  - stone
  - runes
  - oaths
  - ancestral records
  - forge mechanisms
  - volcanic pressure
  - warded doors
  - anvils
  - legal truth
- It should feel architectural, ancestral, contractual, forge-bound, practical, durable, serious, memory-heavy, oath-heavy, difficult to move, and terrifying once engaged.

## Dain's Divergence
- A respectable Truthammer wizard or forge-mage might study warding geometry, volcanic pressure runes, defensive abjuration, stone memory, legal divination, forge transmutation, structural conjuration, ancestral histories, anti-intrusion glyphs, and oath-bound doors.
- Dain studies illusion, disguise, strange conjuration, absurd shelters, cold garments, useless spell residue, magical edge cases, and impressions no serious dwarf respects.
- Possible thesis: the Truthammers taught Dain that magic could be stored in stone, oath, forge, and mountain; [Garl Glittergold](../../Lore/Garl%20Glittergold.md)'s book taught him that magic could also be stored in jokes, weather, embarrassment, wetness, and unfinished edges.

## Open Questions

- How much skill did Dain actually have?
- Did Dain leave before mastery, fail a rite, abandon the path, or get expelled from it?
- Did the forge accident, if used, happen during his apprenticeship?
- Which parts does Dain remember fondly, resent, hide, or distort into jokes?
