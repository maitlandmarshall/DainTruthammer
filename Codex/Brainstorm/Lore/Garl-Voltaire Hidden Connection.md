# Garl-Voltaire Hidden Connection

## One-Line Summary
A DM-only, non-canon idea that Garl Glittergold may be the golden benevolent trickster aspect of a future ascended Voltaire, hinted through marginalia rather than stated.

## Core Secret Idea
- [Garl Glittergold](../../Lore/Garl%20Glittergold.md) may secretly be a divine aspect of Voltaire after ascension, or one distilled side of Voltaire's future godhood.
- The idea is not that Garl is openly Voltaire.
- The idea is not that Dain knows this.
- Garl would represent the cleaner, more worshippable, golden aspect of Voltaire's divine duality.

## The Aspect Embodies
- cleverness
- protective deception
- useful uselessness
- divine jokes
- hidden utility
- misdirection used for good
- reality rewritten through play
- small absurdities that become sacred tools

## Possible Hints
- The Inephemeral Scribe.
- Faint author attribution as `V.` or `Vol-`.
- Rewritten title pages.
- Gold ink.
- Self-contradictory marginal notes.
- Suspicious insistence that the author is definitely Garl.
- `Property of Absolutely Not a God`.
- Bookish apotheosis themes.
- Formal manic logic.
- Divine duality motifs.
- Sun, gold, masks, signatures, and branding jokes.

## What To Avoid
- Directly naming Voltaire in-world too early.
- Obvious full-name references.
- Too much overt self-worship language.
- Anything that makes the secret too blatant.
- Using this page as player-facing or character-facing truth.

## Working Summary
> Garl Glittergold is the golden divine mask of Voltaire's future ascension: the part of him that turned self-worship, cosmic literacy, and catastrophic gamesmanship into benevolent trickery, protective misdirection, and the holy art of useless things.

## Connection To The Book
- The clearest subtle vehicle is [On the Productive Use of Useless Things](./On%20the%20Productive%20Use%20of%20Useless%20Things.md).
- The book can carry the hints through title-page anomalies, gold ink, suspicious author attributions, and commentary by the Inephemeral Scribe.
- This keeps the secret as texture rather than an immediate reveal.
