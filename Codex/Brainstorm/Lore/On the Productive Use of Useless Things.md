- *G. Glittergold*
- Patron of Clever Doors, Responsible Misplacement, Improperly Folded Truths, and Several Ideas That Were Better In Theory.

## Core Thesis
> Useful magic is guarded.
> Useless magic is ignored.
> Therefore, useless magic gets in.

## Proposed Function
- The book may be the first object that pushes Dain toward becoming a living impression reservoir.
- Reading it near the volcanic Truthammer forge may teach him, accidentally, to store impressions instead of ordinary spell energy.
- Possible stored impressions include wet stone, forge heat, mountain cold, quenching steam, oath pressure, embarrassment, family disappointment, suppressed laughter, and useless spell residue.
- If approved, this book could become the lore root for [Truthsage Mode](../Powers/Truthsage%20Mode.md).
