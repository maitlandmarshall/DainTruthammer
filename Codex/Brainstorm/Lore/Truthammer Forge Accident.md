# Truthammer Forge Accident

## One-Line Summary
A non-canon, idea-stage explanation for Dain's exile: a stored-impression discharge made the ancestral forge wrong, and the scandal was that the mountain understood the joke.

## Public Version
- Dain was cast out because of his faith in [Garl Glittergold](../../Lore/Garl%20Glittergold.md).
- This remains the only confirmed cause in the archive. [Confirmed]

## Private Version Idea
- Dain may have accidentally discharged stored impression-magic through an ancestral Truthammer forge or ward system during a solemn forging rite.
- The event did not simply explode.
- Explosion would have been too normal, too honest, and too easy for dwarves to understand.
- Instead, the forge became wrong.

## Possible Effects
- Volcanic channels breathed cold mountain wind.
- Quenching water ran upward.
- Sacred anvils rang with laughter.
- The Oath Hall leaked from the inside.
- A sealed ancestral door opened, apologized, and shut itself.
- A masterwork blade emerged flawless, balanced, and completely unable to cut anything meaningful.
- An ancestral record amended itself to include: "Uselessness, when properly made, is still craft."
- The mountain briefly recognized uselessness as a valid Truthammer craft principle.

## Dramatic Thesis
> Dain was not exiled because he failed.
> He was exiled because, for one terrible moment, the forge understood him.

## Cultural Impact
- Elders might call the event contamination.
- Dain might call it an accident.
- [Garl Glittergold](../../Lore/Garl%20Glittergold.md), if involved, might call it a first draft.
- Dain's father might have remained silent, defended him, condemned him, or been unable to decide. [To verify]
- The wound is not just damage to property; it is damage to the Truthammer idea of seriousness, permanence, and proper craft.

## Connections To Other Ideas
- The accident may follow Dain reading [On the Productive Use of Useless Things](./On%20the%20Productive%20Use%20of%20Useless%20Things.md) near the volcanic forge.
- It may explain why [Truthsage Mode](../Powers/Truthsage%20Mode.md) feels like dwarven ward-storage routed through useless magic.
- It may connect to [Dain as Smith Apprentice](./Dain%20as%20Smith%20Apprentice.md), especially if Dain was expected to become a master smith.
- It may clarify why [Truthhammer Mountains](../../Places/Truthhammer%20Mountains.md) lore matters to Dain's magic and exile.
- It is superseded where incompatible by the accepted [Azer Spark and Truthhammer Fire Portal](../../Lore/Azer%20Spark%20and%20Truthhammer%20Fire%20Portal.md) account.
