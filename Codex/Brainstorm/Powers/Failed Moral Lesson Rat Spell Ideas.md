# Failed Moral Lesson Rat Spell Ideas

## One-Line Summary
Non-canon spell candidates for Dain's new idea, inspired when a rat fell from the ceiling after he prayed to Glittergold about a failed pub moral lesson.

## Status
- [Character-only] [To verify] Idea-stage only, captured from 2026-06-20 live notes.
- Confirmed canon: Dain had the inspiration and began writing in his wizard book / spellbook.
- Not canon: spell name, level, school, components, mechanics, balance, and whether the falling rat has supernatural meaning.

## Brainstorm Routing
- Current shelf: [Brainstorm](../README.md).
- Proposed canon shelf if approved: `Codex/Powers/<chosen spell name>.md`.
- Promotion requirement: explicit user/DM approval of the name, level, school, components, effect, balance, spellbook status, and whether this becomes a known/prepared spell or only research.

## Canon Seed
- The morning after the pub dice scene, Dain reflects on a failed moral lesson.
- Dain spends the morning praying to [Garl Glittergold](../../Lore/Garl%20Glittergold.md) about his disappointment in failing that lesson.
- A rat falls from the ceiling.
- Dain gets inspiration for a spell involving a failed moral lesson and a rat.
- Dain begins writing the beginnings of the new spell in his wizard book / spellbook.

## Design Constraints
- The spell should feel interesting but essentially useless until Dain uses it cleverly.
- It should correct pride, hypocrisy, or ignored lessons without dominating a target's whole will.
- It should lean social, utility, divination, conjuration, or comic-control rather than damage.
- It should involve rats physically or symbolically.
- It should stay table-friendly around consent, party conflict, and moral pressure.
- The best version probably asks Dain to admit his own failed lesson before trying to impose a lesson on someone else.

## Spell Direction 1: Glittergold's Falling Footnote
- Level: cantrip or 1st-level conjuration/enchantment. [To verify]
- Effect: A harmless rat drops from a ceiling, shelf, pocket, hat, or other mildly impossible place and draws attention to a failed lesson. If Dain first names his own failure aloud, one creature involved in that lesson makes a Wisdom save. On a failure, the creature has disadvantage on its next Deception check to pretend nothing happened, or Dain gains `+1d4` on one Insight or Persuasion check about the lesson.
- Useless twist: If there is no real lesson, the rat eats a crumb and leaves.
- Table pitch: "I summon the little footnote Garl sends when a lesson failed to land."

## Spell Direction 2: Truthammer's Necessary Rat
- Level: 1st-level conjuration. [To verify]
- Effect: Dain conjures one ordinary-looking rat in an unoccupied space near a creature that recently ignored a moral warning. The rat points, squeaks, or drags a tiny object toward the contradiction. Until the start of Dain's next turn, the target cannot willingly repeat the same petty dishonest act unless it succeeds on a Wisdom save.
- Useless twist: The spell cannot punish major crimes, expose secrets, or prove guilt; it only interrupts a repeated small wrong that Dain personally witnessed.
- Table pitch: "Not justice. Just a rat that insists the bit was understood."

## Spell Direction 3: The Lesson Under The Floorboards
- Level: 1st-level divination or ritual. [To verify]
- Effect: A spectral or conjured rat scurries up to 30 feet toward the nearest clue, object, person, or threshold tied to a recent unresolved minor wrongdoing or failed moral lesson.
- Useless twist: If Dain casts it greedily or to avoid his own fault, the rat leads him to evidence of his own mistake.
- Table pitch: "The lesson was not lost. It crawled under something."

## Spell Direction 4: Conjure Unhelpful Witness
- Level: 1st-level conjuration. [To verify]
- Effect: Dain conjures a fey, celestial, or ordinary-seeming rat that witnessed the failed lesson. It can repeat one short phrase it heard or pantomime one action from the scene, but cannot explain motive or context.
- Useless twist: The rat is literal, food-motivated, and pedantic; it may emphasize crumbs, dropped coins, or a chair leg instead of the moral point.
- Table pitch: "A witness, yes. A useful witness, absolutely not."

## Spell Direction 5: Ratify
- Level: cantrip or 1st-level enchantment. [To verify]
- Effect: A rat appears as a witness to a very small promise. For 1 minute, if a willing creature breaks that trivial promise, the rat squeaks loudly and points at them. The spell does not compel, punish, or reveal hidden intent.
- Useless twist: The promise must be modest, immediate, and worth less than `1 gp`, or the rat refuses jurisdiction.
- Table pitch: "By the authority invested in this rat, we all heard you."

## Best Table Pitch
- Lead candidate: `Glittergold's Falling Footnote`.
- Proposed shape: 1st-level conjuration/enchantment, non-damaging, social-control utility.
- Core ask to Tom: "Can Dain research a small rat spell that only works after he admits his own failed lesson, then gives a minor social nudge or exposes one immediate contradiction?"
- Balance target: stronger than a pure joke, weaker than `Suggestion`, and not useful as a combat lock.

## Open Questions
- Is this a cantrip, 1st-level spell, ritual, downtime research seed, or no formal mechanic yet?
- Is the rat real, illusory, fey, celestial, ordinary, or deliberately ambiguous?
- Does the spell require a rat hair, crumb, copper coin, tiny moral ledger, or dropped die as a material component?
- Should it use a Wisdom save, Charisma save, contested Insight/Deception, or no save?
- Can it affect party members, or should it require a willing/non-hostile/social target?
- What counts as a "failed moral lesson"?
- Does Dain have to confess his own failure for the spell to work?
- Does the falling rat imply Glittergold's attention, ordinary tavern decay, or both?
