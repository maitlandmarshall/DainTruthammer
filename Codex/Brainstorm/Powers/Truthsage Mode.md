# Truthsage Mode

## One-Line Summary
A non-canon, idea-stage custom state where Dain stores ambient impressions and channels them through physical attacks as condensed facets of his useless-magic research.

## Core Concept
- Dain's useless magic might store ambient impressions rather than ordinary spell energy.
- Possible impressions include wet stone, mountain wind, volcanic forge heat, cold vents, oath pressure, embarrassment, failed spell residue, suppressed laughter, fear, disappointment, almost-truth, and the moment a serious thing becomes accidentally funny.
- Dain gathers impressions through meditation, observation, exposure to strange conditions, and failed or impractical spell research.
- The state is imagined as a hybrid of:
  - dwarven ward storage
  - forge magic
  - Garl-inspired trickster theology
  - Dain's useless-spell research
  - anime-style outside-energy gathering
  - stored preparation released through the body rather than normal spellcasting

## Draft Mechanical Frame
- During a long rest, Dain meditates for 10 minutes on ambient impressions from the world around him.
- He stores a number of Impressions, possibly equal to proficiency bonus. [To verify]
- As a bonus action, Dain may enter Truthsage Mode for 1 minute.
- While in Truthsage Mode:
  - Dain cannot cast leveled spells. [To verify whether cantrips remain allowed]
  - Dain may use Intelligence for attack and damage rolls with simple weapons, smithing hammers, quarterstaffs, daggers, or unarmed strikes. [To verify weapon list]
  - His weapon and unarmed attacks count as magical.
  - Once per turn when he hits a creature with a physical attack, he may spend 1 stored Impression to trigger a Facet from one of his approved useless spells.
  - Facets use Dain's spell save DC.
  - The mode ends early if Dain is incapacitated.
- Optional desperation rule: if Dain enters Truthsage Mode with no spell slots remaining, he gains temporary hit points equal to wizard level + Intelligence modifier. [To verify]

## Design Goal
- This should give Dain strange backup utility when spell slots run low.
- It should not turn Dain into a raw martial damage dealer.
- The mode should feel like a weird wizard solving combat through edge cases, sensory residue, and weaponized bad premises.
- Scaling should come from DM-approved useless-spell research rather than free normal spellcasting.

## Facet Scaling Idea
- Each new DM-approved useless spell may unlock a related Facet.
- A Facet is not the full spell; it is a condensed physical expression of the spell's concept delivered through a hit.
- Possible scaling levers:
  - Impressions equal proficiency bonus per long rest.
  - More approved useless spells unlock more Facet options.
  - Higher-level useless spells may cost more Impressions.
  - Stronger combo effects require further research or DM approval.
  - Facets should favor short-duration odd control effects over direct damage.

## Possible Facets

### Wet Thesis
- Source: [Truthammer's Leaky Tent](../../Powers/Truthammer%20Leaky%20Tent.md).
- On hit, Dain spends 1 Impression and the target makes a Constitution saving throw.
- On a failed save, the target becomes Wet until the start of Dain's next turn. [To verify condition wording]
- Flavor premise: dryness was only an assumption.
- Possible line: "You have been reminded of shelter."

### Mountain Inside
- Source: future cold-jacket spell or the existing [Always-Cold Robes](../../Items/Always-Cold%20Robes.md) prototype.
- On hit, Dain spends 1 Impression and the target makes a Constitution saving throw.
- On a failed save:
  - target's speed is reduced by 10 feet
  - target cannot take reactions until the start of Dain's next turn
- Stronger combo possibility: if the target is already Wet, cold-vulnerable, or exposed to magical cold, the failed save could impose a stronger one-round control effect. [To verify; likely high risk for balance]
- Possible line: "The jacket was always optional. The lesson less so."

### Father's Correction
- Source: possible future backstory spell or emotional impression, not an existing formal spell.
- On hit, Dain spends 1 Impression and the target makes a Wisdom saving throw.
- On a failed save, the target briefly experiences the crushing disappointment of a master smith who expected better because better was possible.
- Possible effects:
  - target cannot take reactions until the start of Dain's next turn
  - target has disadvantage on its next attack
  - target briefly loses composure
- This is funny, but emotionally loaded. It should not become canon without approval of the father/backstory premise.

### Door-Feeling
- Source: possible future door, threshold, or mistaken-entrance spell.
- On hit, Dain spends 1 Impression and the target makes a Charisma or Strength saving throw.
- Possible failed-save effects:
  - target is pushed 5 or 10 feet
  - target blurts out a short honest phrase
  - target becomes briefly convinced it is on the wrong side of an important threshold
- Possible line: "The door knows."

### Forge That Refuses The Premise
- Source: possible future forge-related spell or backstory expression.
- On hit, Dain spends 1 Impression and the target makes a Constitution or Wisdom saving throw.
- Possible failed-save effects:
  - a metal weapon becomes awkward for one attack
  - target has disadvantage on its next weapon attack
  - worn metal briefly becomes uncomfortable
  - weapon remembers it was once ore and briefly reconsiders
