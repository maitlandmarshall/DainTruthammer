# Brainstorm

## One-Line Summary
Staging shelf for non-canon ideas that may become Codex lore, powers, items, events, or rules only after explicit approval.

## Purpose
- Keep brainstorms searchable without letting them masquerade as confirmed campaign truth.
- Preserve useful speculative packets, mechanical drafts, backstory variants, and DM-only possibilities.
- Make promotion into the proper Codex shelf a deliberate workflow rather than an accident.

## Structure
- `Codex/Brainstorm/Lore/` for speculative lore, backstory, cosmology, and mysteries.
- `Codex/Brainstorm/Powers/` for proposed mechanics, spell ideas, boons, modes, and features.
- `Codex/Brainstorm/Items/` for proposed items or artifacts.
- `Codex/Brainstorm/Characters/`, `Places/`, `Factions/`, `Events/`, and `Rules/` may be added when needed.

## Required Page Markings
Every brainstorm page should include:
- `## Status` with `[DM-private]` or other knowledge tags and a clear `Idea-stage only` note.
- `## Brainstorm Routing` with:
  - current shelf
  - proposed canon shelf if approved
  - promotion requirement
- `## Open Questions` or equivalent approval questions.
- `## Related Entries` linking both canon anchors and other brainstorm pages.

## Promotion Workflow
1. Get explicit user and/or DM approval for what becomes true.
2. Decide the knowledge boundary: [Party], [Character-only], [DM-private], [Inferred], [Rumor], or [To verify].
3. Move or split approved material into the proper canonical shelf: `Codex/Lore/`, `Codex/Powers/`, `Codex/Items/`, etc.
4. Rewrite status sections so approved facts become confirmed, partial approvals stay marked, and rejected or unused ideas remain in Brainstorm.
5. Update canonical pages affected by the approval, such as [Dain Truthammer](../Characters/Dain%20Truthammer.md), [Current State](../../Character/Current%20State.md), [Spellbook](../../Character/Spellbook.md), [Inventory](../../Character/Inventory.md), or [Relationships](../../Character/Relationships.md).
6. Update [Open Threads](../Lore/Open%20Threads.md): mark approval questions resolved, active, or deferred.
7. Generate and embed any required canonical image only after the subject is approved, unless the user explicitly wants concept art.
8. Commit the promoted packet once links, state changes, and images are clean.

## Guardrails
- Do not cite Brainstorm pages as canon during lore lookup unless the answer explicitly says the material is non-canon.
- Do not update character state, inventory, relationships, or timeline from a Brainstorm page without approval.
- Do not place idea-stage pages directly in `Codex/Lore/`, `Codex/Powers/`, or other canonical shelves.
- If a brainstorm is partly approved, split it: approved pieces move to the canonical shelf; unapproved pieces stay here with links.
- DM-only secrets can stay here or move to `Module/` if they should not be treated as player-facing Codex material.
