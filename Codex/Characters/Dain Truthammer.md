Dain believes his continued life was purchased at a cost he can never repay.

He refuses to surrender the family name; instead, he tries to re-forge its meaning so that strangers remember `Truthammer` for kindness, hospitality, and goodwill rather than his exile.
- [Character-only] Giving `Glittergold's Share` directly to gnomes is therefore devotion and quiet defiance at once.
