# Creek-Fork Ogre Ambush

![Creek-fork ogre ambush comic page](../../Adventures/2026-04-18/2026-04-18_comic-page-02_creek-fork-ogres.png)

## One-Line Summary
Two ogres attack the rainy creek-fork camp during Dain's watch, and one is compelled to lie face down in the creek and float away.

## Status
- [Party] [Confirmed] Occurred on 2026-04-18.
- [Party] [Confirmed] The party kills both ogres and survives the ambush.

## Key Beats
- The Astral Elf's owl spots the ogres and alerts the party.
- Dain casts `Sleep`, but the DM requires the 2014 ruleset; Dain rolls `32` and the spell affects neither ogre.
- Dain ends up prone in the river during the opening confusion.
- Dain casts `Suggestion` on one ogre, ordering it to lie down in the creek with its arms behind its head.
- The ogre drops its club, obeys, and slowly floats downstream face down.
- After the fight, Dain stands at the Y of the creek fork and gives a theatrical post-battle speech while the party scavenges the ogres.
