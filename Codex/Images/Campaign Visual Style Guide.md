# Campaign Visual Style Guide

## Canon Style
**Kinetic Shonen Fantasy Anime**: a clean modern battle-anime fantasy style with strong line art, expressive character acting, bold silhouettes, sharp motion, and smooth cel-shaded color.

## Core Look
- Use elegant, confident anime linework with varied line weight, clean silhouettes, and readable facial expressions.
- Favor shonen battle composition: dynamic poses, strong action arcs, speed lines, impact frames, expressive hands, and clear staged panel readability.
- Keep the mood adventurous, intense, clever, and emotionally vivid rather than whimsical or cozy.
- Use stylized fantasy anime anatomy rather than photorealism: grounded body structure, heightened expressions, crisp shapes, and heroic exaggeration where action calls for it.
- Render color with smooth cel-shaded forms, clean flats, crisp highlights, and selective atmospheric lighting.
- Avoid grainy texture, heavy watercolor wash, gouache roughness, painterly fuzz, and storybook softness unless specifically requested.

## Color And Light
- Signature magic: warm gold, candle amber, and soft ivory sparkles, especially for [Garl Glittergold](../Lore/Garl%20Glittergold.md), illusion, conjuration, and Dain's tricks.
- World shadows: cool blue, mist grey, pine green, stone charcoal, and rain-washed slate.
- Accent colors: moss green, tarnished brass, parchment cream, burgundy wax seals, electric spell glyphs, and high-contrast aura light.
- Use stronger anime contrast when action peaks: rim lights, sharp highlights, clean shadow shapes, and punchy magical energy.
- Avoid muddy all-brown images, heavy photoreal grit, glossy digital plastic, single-hue palettes, and noisy film grain.

## Line Art Rules
- Line art should remain visible and beautiful at normal markdown reading size.
- Use confident contour lines, elegant interior lines, expressive hair and beard shapes, and small decorative details where they support character.
- Prefer clean line economy over dense crosshatching; shadows should usually be cel-shaded rather than grain-rendered.
- Avoid over-rendering faces into realism; preserve sharp expression, readability, and dramatic anime acting.
- No generated text, captions, speech balloons, logos, or watermarks unless explicitly requested.

## Dain Truthammer Visual Anchors
- Male dwarf wizard, age 107, 4'9", compact and sturdy build.
- White skin, brown eyes, black hair, full black beard with subtle grey or warm highlights.
- Mischievous, benevolent expression: clever eyes, knowing half-smile, kind but dangerous when defending others.
- Slightly naturally disheveled, as if scholarship and travel keep tugging his dignity sideways, but framed with sharper anime confidence during decisive moments.
- Fancy scholar's robes, travel-worn but still elegant, with [Garl Glittergold](../Lore/Garl%20Glittergold.md)'s sigil worked into clasp, brooch, embroidery, or charm.
- Common props: open spellbook, wand or staff, parchment notes, odd little spell components, hidden-door motifs, warm golden illusion sparks, clean aura flares, and crisp spell geometry.

## Scene Composition
- Adventure panels should feel like stills from a high-quality shonen fantasy episode or theatrical animation sequence: readable action, strong foreground shapes, and clear story beats.
- Use environmental framing: tent flaps, stone arches, trees, rain, smoke, spell rings, doorways, or maps can frame the subject.
- For strange magic, show precise animated effects: clean glyph rings, golden aura sparks, sharp conjuration geometry, speed-line distortions, and elegant visual jokes that still respect the canon.
- Keep injuries, monsters, and danger stylized rather than gory unless the user asks otherwise.
- Use action poses with readable silhouettes before adding detail; the viewer should understand the scene at thumbnail size.

## Prompt Prefix
Use this style block for future image prompts unless the user overrides it:

```text
Kinetic Shonen Fantasy Anime style: clean modern battle-anime fantasy illustration, beautiful visible line art with varied line weight, expressive character acting, bold readable silhouettes, dynamic action arcs, speed-line energy, smooth cel-shaded color, crisp highlights, warm golden magical flourishes, high-contrast cinematic lighting, no photorealism, no generated text, no watermark.
```

## Prompt Avoids
```text
Avoid photorealism, muddy realism, generic dark fantasy grit, plastic 3D rendering, watercolor wash, gouache texture, visible grain, painterly fuzz, cozy storybook whimsy, indistinct faces, unreadable tiny details, horror gore, text, captions, speech balloons, logos, watermarks, named living-artist imitation, franchise character likenesses, and random style drift from established character references.
```

## Reference Continuity
- Use existing codex portraits and scene panels as identity and costume references.
- Preserve recurring silhouettes, color motifs, iconic props, and relationship to warm gold magic.
- If a subject has no image yet, generate a clean reference image before relying on it in crowded scenes.
