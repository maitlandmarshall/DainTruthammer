# Campaign Visual Style Guide

## Canon Style
**Whimsical Inked Fantasy**: a comic and manga-inspired fantasy style with beautiful line art, expressive character acting, playful magical flourishes, and clear silhouettes.

## Core Look
- Use elegant ink linework with varied line weight, clean silhouettes, and readable facial expressions.
- Favor comic-book and manga composition: dynamic poses, staged panel clarity, expressive hands, and slightly theatrical character acting.
- Keep the mood whimsical, clever, and curious rather than grim, even when scenes are dangerous.
- Use stylized fantasy realism rather than photorealism: grounded anatomy, but with heightened expressions, crisp shapes, and visual charm.
- Render color with clean cel-shaded forms, soft watercolor or gouache texture, and selective painterly atmosphere.

## Color And Light
- Signature magic: warm gold, candle amber, and soft ivory sparkles, especially for Garl Glittergold, illusion, conjuration, and Dain's tricks.
- World shadows: cool blue, mist grey, pine green, stone charcoal, and rain-washed slate.
- Accent colors: moss green, tarnished brass, parchment cream, burgundy wax seals, and luminous spell glyphs.
- Avoid muddy all-brown images, heavy photoreal grit, glossy digital plastic, and single-hue palettes.

## Line Art Rules
- Line art should remain visible and beautiful at normal markdown reading size.
- Use confident contour lines, fine hatching, expressive hair and beard shapes, and small decorative details where they support character.
- Avoid over-rendering faces into realism; preserve charm, readability, and expression.
- No generated text, captions, speech balloons, logos, or watermarks unless explicitly requested.

## Dain Truthammer Visual Anchors
- Male dwarf wizard, age 107, 4'9", compact and sturdy build.
- White skin, brown eyes, black hair, full black beard with subtle grey or warm highlights.
- Mischievous, benevolent expression: clever eyes, knowing half-smile, kind but dangerous when defending others.
- Slightly naturally disheveled, as if scholarship and travel keep tugging his dignity sideways.
- Fancy scholar's robes, travel-worn but still elegant, with Garl Glittergold's sigil worked into clasp, brooch, embroidery, or charm.
- Common props: open spellbook, wand or staff, parchment notes, odd little spell components, hidden-door motifs, warm golden illusion sparks.

## Scene Composition
- Adventure panels should feel like pages from a high-quality fantasy comic: readable action, strong foreground shapes, and clear story beats.
- Use environmental framing: tent flaps, stone arches, trees, rain, smoke, spell rings, doorways, or maps can frame the subject.
- For strange magic, show whimsical effects with curling glyphs, tiny impossible lights, visual puns, or elegant nonsense that still respects the canon.
- Keep injuries, monsters, and danger stylized rather than gory unless the user asks otherwise.

## Prompt Prefix
Use this style block for future image prompts unless the user overrides it:

```text
Whimsical Inked Fantasy style: comic-book and manga-inspired fantasy illustration, beautiful visible line art with varied line weight, expressive character acting, clean silhouettes, dynamic but readable composition, warm golden magical flourishes, soft cel-shaded color with light watercolor/gouache texture, charming and curious tone, no photorealism, no generated text, no watermark.
```

## Prompt Avoids
```text
Avoid photorealism, muddy realism, generic dark fantasy grit, plastic 3D rendering, indistinct faces, unreadable tiny details, horror gore, text, captions, speech balloons, logos, watermarks, and random style drift from established character references.
```

## Reference Continuity
- Use existing codex portraits and scene panels as identity and costume references.
- Preserve recurring silhouettes, color motifs, iconic props, and relationship to warm gold magic.
- If a subject has no image yet, generate a clean reference image before relying on it in crowded scenes.
