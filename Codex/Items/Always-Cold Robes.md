# Always-Cold Robes

![Always-Cold Robes prototype](../../Adventures/2026-05-03/2026-05-03_dain-pivots-to-always-cold-robes.png)

## One-Line Summary
An in-progress Dain Truthammer robe prototype that pivots away from practical warmth and toward the gloriously inconvenient promise of always making the wearer cold.
