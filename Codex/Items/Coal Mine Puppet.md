# Coal Mine Puppet

![Coal Mine Puppet reference](./Coal_Mine_Puppet_ref.png)

## One-Line Summary
A strange little puppet carried by one of the [Southern Human Traders](../Factions/Southern%20Human%20Traders.md), apparently able to interfere with [Dain Truthammer](../Characters/Dain%20Truthammer.md)'s attempt to cast `Identify`.

## Status
- [Party] [Confirmed] First appears on 2026-05-03 when a human trader sighs and pulls it out after [Kagrenac](../Characters/The%20Astral%20Elf.md) warns Dain in Draconic that the newcomers are glowing with magic.
- [Party] [Confirmed] Dain holds out his hand, shakes the puppet's hand, and attempts to cast `Identify`.
- [Character-only] [Inferred] The puppet seems to prevent Dain from casting or completing `Identify`.
- [Party] [Confirmed] The puppet speaks in a high voice and says, "unless you can take me back to the coal mines."
