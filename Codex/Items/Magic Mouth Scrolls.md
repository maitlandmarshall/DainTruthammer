## Why Dain Wanted Them
- `Magic Mouth` is a 2nd-level illusion ritual built around programmable triggers, conditional behaviour and a lasting magical message.
- Those principles directly support Dain's long-term research into spells that do one peculiar thing under unnecessarily exact circumstances.
- The original five copies offered opportunities for transcription, field use and controlled experimentation; none remain now.
