# Mask of Changed Appearance

![Mask of Changed Appearance reference](./Mask_of_Changed_Appearance_ref.png)

## One-line summary
A jeweled magical mask with 3 charges that can invisibly change the wearer's facial appearance.

## Status
- [Party] [Confirmed] Found inside the [Goblin Camp Chest](./Goblin%20Camp%20Chest.md) on 2026-05-03.
- [Party] [Confirmed] The chest also contained `500 gp`.
- [Party] [Confirmed] The mask has `3` charges.
- [Party] [Confirmed] As a magical action, the wearer can spend `1` charge to change their face's appearance.
- [Party] [Confirmed] The change can smooth or deepen wrinkles and hide or accentuate bags under the wearer's eyes.
- [Party] [Confirmed] While the wearer's appearance is changed, the mask cannot be seen.
