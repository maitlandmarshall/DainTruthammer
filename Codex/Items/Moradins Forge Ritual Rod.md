# Moradin's Forge Ritual Rod

![Dain holds the cooling ritual rod as his skin takes on a bronze sheen](../../Adventures/2026-08-29/2026-08-29_dain-performs-moradin-forge-ritual.png)

## One-Line Summary
An unidentified rod used in Moradin's shared-forge ritual that grows colder in Dain's grasp as Dain himself grows hotter.

## Status
- [Party] [Confirmed on 2026-08-29] Dain takes the rod while performing the ritual inspired by Taleesh and the brief azer vision.
- [Party] [Confirmed] The rod becomes colder while Dain becomes hotter and his skin develops a bronze sheen.
- [Party] [Confirmed] Dain puts the rod back down; it is not recorded as acquired or carried.
- [To verify] Material, size, original temperature, owner, purpose, exact ritual sequence, temperature after release and whether it is the same object produced from another worshipper's metal offering.

## What Dain Knows
- [Character-only] Holding the rod causes or coincides with an inverse temperature change: the rod cools while Dain heats.
- [Character-only] After releasing it, Dain feels intense religious guilt over not tending the Truthammer forge.
- [Inferred] The rod may interact with Dain's half-Azer connection, but no cause has been confirmed.

## Properties
- The rod grows colder while Dain holds it. [Confirmed observation]
- [To verify] Whether the effect occurs for anyone, only ritual participants, only believers, or specifically Dain.
- [To verify] Whether it stores, exchanges, redirects or merely symbolizes heat.
- [To verify] Whether the bronze sheen and guilt originate from the rod, forge, Moradin, the azer, Dain's heritage or Dain's own conscience.
