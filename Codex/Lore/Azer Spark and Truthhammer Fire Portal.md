# Azer Spark and Truthhammer Fire Portal

![Dain accepts the azer connection during Moradin's forge ritual](../../Adventures/2026-08-29/2026-08-29_dain-performs-moradin-forge-ritual.png)

## One-Line Summary
The accepted origin of Dain's azer connection: fire-plane magic entered his story through a forge incident that opened a portal beneath Truthammer, while azer creation begins with an elemental spark placed into a crafted bronze shell.

## Status
- [Character-only] [Confirmed by user on 2026-08-29] Dain's ritual in Moradin's chamber was his acceptance of the azer connection offered in the DM's conditional lore.
- [Confirmed from DM wording accepted by user] Dain received a measure of magic from the Elemental Plane of Fire.
- [Confirmed from DM wording accepted by user] The forge beneath Truthammer has an open portal to the Elemental Plane of Fire that appeared when Dain had “the incident.”
- [Confirmed from DM wording] Azer creation begins with a dwarf-shaped bronze shell and a transfer of part of the creator's elemental flame and life force.
- [Incomplete source] The supplied creation explanation ends at `The Result:` without its concluding text. The completed result and its application to Dain remain [To verify].

## Canon Boundary
- Dain accepted an azer connection; this is no longer an unused proposal.
- The portal and transferred fire-plane magic are established as part of that accepted connection.
- [To verify] Whether Dain was literally created through the bronze-shell process, descended from such a being, transformed by the incident, or connected in another way.
- [To verify] What Dain knew before the ritual versus what he now understands intuitively.

## Established Elements
- Dain received a portion of magic from the Elemental Plane of Fire.
- The forge beneath Truthammer contains an open portal to the Elemental Plane of Fire.
- The portal appeared when Dain had “the incident,” understood as the forge incident; the exact event remains [To verify].
- An azer crafts a dwarf-shaped bronze outer shell.
- The azer transfers part of its own inner elemental flame and life force into the bronze body.
- The result of that transfer is missing from the supplied message and must not be invented.

## Established Connections
- [Confirmed] Dain and/or the Truthhammers have a half-Azer connection; the ritual confirms Dain's personal acceptance, while the connection's scope across the clan remains unresolved.
- [Confirmed in play] Dain briefly sees an [azer in Moradin's forge](../Characters/Azer%20in%20Moradins%20Forge.md), then performs the ritual and develops heat, a bronze sheen and guilt about neglecting the Truthammer forge.
- [Confirmed by user] Performing that ritual was Dain accepting the azer connection.
- The accepted portal account supersedes incompatible details in the earlier idea-stage [Truthhammer Forge Accident](../Brainstorm/Lore/Truthammer%20Forge%20Accident.md); compatible details remain proposals only.
