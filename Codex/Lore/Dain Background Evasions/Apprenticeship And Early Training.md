# Apprenticeship And Early Training

## One-line summary
Answers about how Dain learned his craft before he became the wizard he is now.

## Vague Answers
- "I trained under people who prized precision and under others who prized results. I kept both lessons and annoyed both camps."
- "My apprenticeship involved a great many books and a suspicious number of locked cupboards."
- "I was taught formally, corrected sharply, and improved mostly by curiosity."
- "There was a proper tutor for the official parts and several improper ones for the useful parts."
- "I learned early that rules are often just techniques written in a stern hand."
- "My training was excellent once one separates method from supervision."

## Follow-Up Deflections
- "The list of instructors is longer than their patience would justify."
- "Any apprentice worth the title survives at least one teacher's disappointment."
- "A craft is never learned from one pair of hands."
