# Father

## One-line summary
Ways Dain can refer to his father without pinning down the man too quickly.

## Vague Answers
- "My father was the sort of dwarf who could make silence sound instructional."
- "He believed in standards, endurance, and the moral value of not whining in hallways."
- "I got my stubbornness from him, though I'd phrase it more artfully."
- "He was not an easy man, but he was a useful one to have in your foundations."
- "He taught me that being correct and being kind are separate crafts."
- "My father preferred done things to said things."

## Follow-Up Deflections
- "You can tell a great deal about a father from what his child refuses to repeat."
- "He is simpler to understand from a distance than he was at the supper table."
- "A man's children usually explain him badly."
