# First Magic And Strange Spells

## One-line summary
Answers about when Dain first discovered magic and why he loves odd spells so much.

## Vague Answers
- "My first useful magic was probably an accident. My first intentional magic was only slightly more respectable."
- "I discovered early that the world is full of people who underestimate a ridiculous spell."
- "Strange magic was the first kind that felt honest to me."
- "Any fool can admire a fireball. It takes character to appreciate the spell no one else has imagined a use for."
- "I liked the spells that looked like jokes and behaved like tools."
- "My taste in magic improved the moment I stopped trusting what looked impressive."

## Follow-Up Deflections
- "The first spell worth remembering is rarely the first one cast."
- "Early magic is mostly embarrassment with occasional illumination."
- "I could tell you how it began, but that would make it sound intentional."

## Canon Notes
- [Confirmed] Dain actively seeks bizarre and impractical magic.
- [To verify] His first spell or first magical incident has not been fixed.
