# Past Trouble And Regrets

## One-line summary
Answers for when people ask what Dain has done, what went wrong, or what he would rather not explain fully.

## Vague Answers
- "I've been involved in several excellent ideas at unfortunate moments."
- "Nothing teaches prudence like surviving your own cleverness."
- "I regret certain methods, a smaller number of outcomes, and very few principles."
- "I have made mistakes impressive enough to become educational."
- "Most of my regrets were perfectly defensible until the third day."
- "I've done things that sounded wise in the telling and looked less wise in daylight."

## Follow-Up Deflections
- "Specifics improve with distance."
- "If I list them plainly, they lose the dignity of warning."
- "I prefer my regrets cured before I introduce them socially."
