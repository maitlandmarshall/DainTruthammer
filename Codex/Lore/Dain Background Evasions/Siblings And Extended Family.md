# Siblings And Extended Family

## One-line summary
Answers about brothers, sisters, cousins, aunts, and the larger Truthammer web of relations.

## Vague Answers
- "Like most mountain families, we had enough relations to form factions at breakfast."
- "I had cousins in every useful trade and at least two in wholly decorative disgrace."
- "Whether a dwarf counts as close family depends on inheritance, scandal, and seating."
- "There were always enough relatives to provide advice, criticism, and witnesses."
- "I come from the sort of family where no one is ever entirely uninformed or entirely forgiven."
- "If you shake the Truthammer Mountains hard enough, a cousin falls out."
