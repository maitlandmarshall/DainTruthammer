# University Or Formal Study

## One-line summary
Ways Dain can answer questions about where and how he studied without naming an institution too cleanly.

## Vague Answers
- "I was educated in the dwarven sense, which is to say thoroughly, unevenly, and by several people who disagreed with one another."
- "There was a hall, certainly. Whether it qualifies as a university depends on how much you value windows."
- "I studied under people who preferred results to banners."
- "It had ledgers, lectures, and at least one corridor no one admitted officially existed. That was education enough."
- "I attended the sort of place that believed a locked archive was merely an advanced invitation."
- "My education was respectable in outline and highly suspicious in detail."

## Follow-Up Deflections
- "The name is less interesting than what they failed to keep out of the library."
- "If I give you the title, you'll assume the curriculum was orderly. It was not."
- "A school may shape a man without deserving all the credit."
