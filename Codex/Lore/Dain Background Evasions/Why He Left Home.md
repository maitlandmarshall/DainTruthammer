# Why He Left Home

## One-line summary
Ways Dain can answer why he left the Truthammer Mountains without committing to one decisive cause.

## Vague Answers
- "I left for the usual reasons: curiosity, principle, and the occasional consequence."
- "Home was good for making me, but less suited to containing me."
- "Some departures are ambitions wearing the clothes of necessity."
- "I had things to learn that were not staying put to be learned."
- "Let us say the mountains taught me enough to leave and not enough to stay quiet."
- "No one leaves home for just one reason worth telling."

## Follow-Up Deflections
- "The honest answer comes in layers, and I only travel with the top few."
- "If I settle on one reason, the others will feel insulted."
- "Departure is always simpler in memory than it was in boots."
