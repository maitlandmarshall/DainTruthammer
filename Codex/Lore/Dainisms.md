# Dainisms

## One-line summary
A growing shelf of Dain Truthammer's sayings, evasions, and polished bits of mountain wisdom.

## Status
- [Character-only] These are in-character sayings Dain can use in conversation, argument, misdirection, or reflection.
- [To verify] Individual sayings may become especially iconic through repeated play.
