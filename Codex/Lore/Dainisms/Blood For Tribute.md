# Blood For Tribute

## Saying
> "You mistake blood for tribute. That is common among small gods and badly made knives."

## Use
- Best used when Dain rejects a cruel being's attempt to turn violence, fear, or sacrifice into worship.
- First recorded after the [Coal Mine Puppet](../../Items/Coal%20Mine%20Puppet.md) stabbed Dain for praying to [Garl Glittergold](../Garl%20Glittergold.md) and denying [Kurtlemack](../Kurtlemack.md) tribute from the winter wolves' deaths.
