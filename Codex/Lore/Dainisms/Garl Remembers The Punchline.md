# Garl Remembers The Punchline

## Dainism
"Careful, little cobalt thing. Garl loves a joke, and worse for you, he remembers the punchline."

## Use
- For a sharp devotional warning after a slight against [Garl Glittergold](../Garl%20Glittergold.md).
- Best delivered calmly, as if Dain is informing the offender of a practical hazard rather than making a threat.
