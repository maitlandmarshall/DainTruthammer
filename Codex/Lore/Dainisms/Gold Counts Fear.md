# Gold Counts Fear

## Dainism
"Gold's just a shiny way to count fear. Spend it, melt it, bury it, and it still won't buy back your name."

## Use
- For dismissing greed, refusing a corrupt bargain, or warning someone who thinks wealth can repair honor.
