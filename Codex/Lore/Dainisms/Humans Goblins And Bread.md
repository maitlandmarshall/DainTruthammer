# Humans, Goblins, And Bread

## One-line summary
Dainisms for persuading wary humans to trust Dain enough to enter the goblin camp and break bread.

## Status
- [Character-only] Table-side prompt sheet for the 2026-05-03 goblin camp negotiation.
- [To verify] These lines are not confirmed spoken in canon unless later used at the table.

## Use Case
- Situation: The humans do not trust the goblins, and Dain is trying to convince them to follow the party into the camp and share a meal.
- Core angle: The humans do not need to trust the goblins immediately; they need to trust Dain's managed risk.

## Dainisms
- "You needn't trust the goblins yet. Trust me for ten minutes, and keep your hands where wisdom can see them."
- "A shared meal is cheaper than a shared battlefield, and I've never known soup to draw first blood."
- "If they wanted us dead, they'd have spent fewer words on tolls."
- "Come in wary if you must, but come in. Suspicion travels better after it has eaten."
- "We are not asking you to embrace them. Only to sit close enough that everyone remembers they have stomachs before they remember they have knives."
- "Bread first. Steel later, if bread proves foolish."
- "A campfire is a small treaty with smoke on top."
- "Let the goblins see guests instead of prey, and let the humans see hosts instead of monsters. If either side forgets, I shall be very disappointed in the quality of civilization today."
- "I have placed myself between trickery and consequence before. Today I am asking you to let me do it again."
- "The road is cold, the wind is worsening, and pride is a miserable blanket. Come eat."
- "No one is asking for friendship. We begin with bread, because bread is harder to misinterpret than a drawn blade."
- "If this fails, you may call me a fool after supper. If it works, you may call me a fool with better timing."
- "Trust is too expensive for strangers. Fine. Lend me caution instead, and I'll return it with interest."
- "They have a leader, a fire, and enough sense to bargain. That is more civilization than many nobles manage before breakfast."
- "Let us make the goblins prove hospitality while watched. That is safer than making them prove hostility while cornered."
