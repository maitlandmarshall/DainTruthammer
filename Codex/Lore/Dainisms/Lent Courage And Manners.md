# Lent Courage And Manners

## Dainism
"A puppet may insult Glittergold, but only because someone else lent it courage and forgot to lend it manners."

## Use
- For responding to a controlled, conjured, mechanical, or otherwise borrowed voice that disrespects [Garl Glittergold](../Garl%20Glittergold.md).
- Especially useful when Dain wants to insult the hidden speaker without pretending the puppet itself is the real threat.
