# Remember The Name Truthhammer

## Saying
> "Remember the name Truthhammer."

## Use
- For leaving a room after making a small public impression, especially after charity, hospitality, or a deliberately memorable useless act.
- The line works as both self-advertisement and a promise that Dain expects the name to matter later.
