# Morndinsamman

## One-Line Summary
The dwarven pantheon honoured across fourteen temples in [Ola Dung Lot](../Places/Ola%20Dung%20Lot.md).

## The Fourteen Gods
1. **Moradin** — greater god, chief among the Morndinsamman, god of creation, crafts and the dwarven race as a whole.
2. **Berronar Truesilver** — intermediate goddess of hearth and home, Moradin's consort and matriarch of the dwarven pantheon.
3. **Dumathoin** — intermediate god of mining, gems and underground exploration.
4. **Sharindlar** — intermediate goddess of fertility, romantic love and healing.
5. **Clangeddin Silverbeard** — god of battle, valor and honor in combat.
6. **Vergadain** — god of trade, wealth, negotiation, luck, trickery and chance.
7. **Dugmaren Brightmantle** — god of scholarship, discovery and invention.
8. **Gorm Gulthyn** — god of vigilance, defense and protection.
9. **Haela Brightaxe** — goddess of luck and battle.
10. **Marthammor Duin** — god of wanderers.
11. **Thard Harr** — god of survival, hunting and nature.
12. **Abbathor** — god of greed.
13. **Hanseath** — god of war, carousing and alcohol.
14. **Tharmekhûl** — demipower of the forge and molten rock, with war as a lesser domain.
