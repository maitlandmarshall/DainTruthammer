# Samryn Torsten

## One-line summary
The wider mountain area containing the city of [Ola Dung Lot](../Places/Ola%20Dung%20Lot.md) and the party's current route.

## Status
- [Confirmed by user on 2026-08-29] Samryn Torsten is the area, not the city.
- [Confirmed] Agland's assigned destination was a city in or near this mountain area.
- [Retcon] Earlier archive entries conflated Samryn Torsten with the city reached by Gondian airship. The city is [Ola Dung Lot](../Places/Ola%20Dung%20Lot.md).
- [Party] The party has travelled through the area, reached Ola Dung Lot, delivered Agland's sealed letter there, and is preparing to depart aboard a [balloon-like skyship](../Places/Party%20Skyship%20-%202026-08-29.md).

## What the character knows
- [Character-only] Dain knows Samryn Torsten as the wider area around Ola Dung Lot.
- [Character-only] Dain promised to help Kagrenac make the mountain journey safely.

## What the party knows
- [Party] Commander Agland directed the party toward Ola Dung Lot in the Samryn Torsten area.
- [Party] The route runs east from Lerdar and then north along the river into the mountains.
- [Party] A later route sketch suggests the travelled line bends inland from the Lerdar / Llurdar area and curves up the river into the mountains. [To verify]
- [Party] The second day of travel was uneventful, and rain began by the end of it.
- [Party] By the end of the second day, the party reached a Y-shaped junction where two creeks meet and the path splits.

## What is uncertain

- [To verify] Whether the area's name also belongs to a mountain, range, province or other geographic feature.
