# Truthammer Tales

## One-line summary
A shelf of campfire stories, family legends, and mountain absurdities Dain can tell about home.

## Status
- [Character-only] These are usable character stories and sayings for Dain's voice at the table.
- [To verify] Individual tales may be true, embellished, inherited, or half-performed for effect.
