# The Bent Stair Of Old Harvak

## One-line summary
A Truthammer mountain story about pride, crooked masonry, and a stair everyone kept because it taught the right lesson.

## Story
"Back home in the Truthammer Mountains, we had a stair cut into the cliff by Old Harvak, a mason of great pride and uneven judgment. Swore he'd carved it perfectly straight. Problem was, every seventh step leaned just a little to the left. Harvak insisted it was the mountain settling. Then his wife pointed out the whole stair bent exactly the way he squinted after lunch ale. We kept it that way for thirty years because, as my uncle said, 'If the lock exists, it means someone hoped to be clever,' and if a stair bends oddly, it means the mountain is trying to teach you where not to rush."
