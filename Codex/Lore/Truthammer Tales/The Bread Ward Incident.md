# The Bread Ward Incident

## One-line summary
A family pantry defense ward that mostly protected the house from its own relatives.

## Story
"My aunt swore her kitchen was protected by an ancestral ward against thieves. She was right, in a manner of speaking. Anyone trying to steal from the pantry triggered a shower of flour, stale crumbs, and one extremely judgmental copper ladle. After a while the ward mostly targeted family members who forgot where they'd put the jam. Still, it worked. No burglar wants to explain why he fled covered in bread ingredients."

## Dainism Used
- [Character-only] A useful reminder that practical magic is often funniest at home.
