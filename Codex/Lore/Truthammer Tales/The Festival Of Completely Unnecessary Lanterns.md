# The Festival Of Completely Unnecessary Lanterns

## One-line summary
A mountain festival dedicated to impossible lantern designs, questionable taste, and one triumphant invisible castle.

## Story
"In my home halls we once had a festival where every household had to hang a lantern shaped like something the mountain had never seen before. There were mushroom lanterns, dragon lanterns, a very poor attempt at a camel, and one unforgettable lantern shaped like tax law. Nobody understood that one, but it frightened the children magnificently. The winner was old Nara, who made a lantern shaped like an invisible castle and insisted it was obviously the best crafted entry there. Hard to argue with that sort of confidence."

## Dainism Used
- [Character-only] Told in the spirit of Dain's love for elegant nonsense and impractical wonders.
