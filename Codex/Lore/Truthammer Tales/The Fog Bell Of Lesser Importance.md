# The Fog Bell Of Lesser Importance

## One-line summary
A thick-fog false alarm that mobilized a whole mountain village against what turned out to be a sheep.

## Story
"There was a bell tower above our home that was only meant to ring for emergencies: avalanche, raiders, fire, or Aunt Belda seeing a 'suspicious weather pattern.' One autumn the fog got so thick nobody could tell whether the bell was ringing or merely thinking about it. So the village spent half a day putting on boots, grabbing axes, kissing loved ones, and assembling in the square for what turned out to be a very lonely sheep sneezing in a drain tunnel. Since then we say, 'A straight road is for people with nothing to hide and nothing to find, but a mountain path will gladly hide a sheep and call it prophecy.'"
