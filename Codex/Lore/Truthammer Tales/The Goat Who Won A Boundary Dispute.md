# The Goat Who Won A Boundary Dispute

## One-line summary
A village land quarrel resolved by one stubborn goat and an older dwarf with better judgment than patience.

## Story
"In the Truthammer Mountains, two cousins argued for eleven years over which one owned a strip of scree between their houses. Measurements, survey pegs, shouted ancestors, the whole ridiculous liturgy. Then one spring a goat wandered up, ate both marker flags, and refused to stand anywhere but the middle. So Granny Hetha declared the land belonged to the goat and charged both cousins grazing tax until they learned shame. That's mountain justice. Kindness with a crooked hat is still kindness."
