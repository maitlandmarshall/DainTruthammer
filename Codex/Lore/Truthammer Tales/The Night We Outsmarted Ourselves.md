# The Night We Outsmarted Ourselves

## One-line summary
A village security plan so clever it succeeded chiefly in confusing its own side.

## Story
"One summer there was a rumor that goblins were sneaking near the lower paths, so the whole village made decoys, false campfires, and fake patrol routes. Very sophisticated. Very cunning. Magnificent use of spare cloaks. In the end we frightened off not goblins, but three merchants, a priest, and our own returning hunting party. Grandfather called it a triumph regardless. Said if your trick fools everyone, it was at least well made."

## Dainism Used
- [Character-only] A family proof that mischief can be respectable if enough planning goes into it.
