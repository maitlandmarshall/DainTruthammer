# The Secret Door That Wasn't

## One-line summary
A grandly engineered hidden passage that led not to treasure, but to pickled beets and justified pride.

## Story
"My people are Truthammers. We are not immune to cleverness. We merely overinvest in it. There was once a hallway in my home hold with a magnificent hidden lever, a turning stone, three false seams, and a carved warning in Old Dwarvish. Everyone assumed it opened a secret chamber full of relics. Turned out it swung open a pantry with pickled beets and emergency candles. My grandfather said that was still a treasure, and besides, there's no such thing as a useless spell, only a dull wizard, and no such thing as a useless secret door if it keeps the beets cool."
