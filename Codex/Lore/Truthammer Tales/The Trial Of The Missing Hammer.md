# The Trial Of The Missing Hammer

## One-line summary
A ceremonial hammer disappears, a family erupts, and the real culprit turns out to be wounded pride and a nap.

## Story
"A smith in the Truthammer Mountains once accused his apprentice of stealing a ceremonial hammer. Whole family in an uproar. Honor insulted, lineage invoked, tea served with lethal restraint. Three days later they found the hammer where the smith himself had hidden it to 'test vigilance,' then forgotten due to a nap. Apprentice was vindicated, smith was mocked for decades, and the family motto became, 'A lie told to a tyrant is just a shortcut, but a lie told to yourself is how you lose your own hammer.'"

## Dainism Used
- "A lie told to a tyrant is just a shortcut."
