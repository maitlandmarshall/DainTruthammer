# The Tunnel Echo Courtship

## One-line summary
A courting custom judged by tunnel acoustics and ruined by an echo with the soul of an accountant.

## Story
"In the Truthammer Mountains, young dwarves used to test their singing voices in a side tunnel called Lover's Bend, because the echo was said to reveal your true character. Strong echo meant courage. Soft echo meant humility. No echo at all meant the tunnel didn't like you. My cousin Brenna brought a suitor there once, and the tunnel threw his love poem back sounding exactly like a tax inventory. She married a sculptor instead. Quite right too."

## Dainism Used
- [Character-only] Best delivered with Dain's suspicion that some forms of respectability are secretly absurd.
