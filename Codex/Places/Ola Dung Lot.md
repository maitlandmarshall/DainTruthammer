# Ola Dung Lot

![Dain prepares to board a balloon-like skyship at Ola Dung Lot](../../Adventures/2026-08-29/2026-08-29_boarding-balloon-skyship.png)

## One-Line Summary
The city in the [Samryn Torsten](../Lore/Samryn%20Torsten.md) area where the party delivered Agland's letter and is now preparing to board its skyship.

## Status
- [Party] [Confirmed] First named in play by the [Southern Human Traders](../Factions/Southern%20Human%20Traders.md) on 2026-05-03.
- [Confirmed by user on 2026-08-29] Ola Dung Lot is the city; Samryn Torsten is the wider area.
- [Retcon] Earlier archive entries interpreted Ola Dung Lot as a high-mountain lake. That interpretation is superseded, though the original 2026-05-03 live prompt remains preserved verbatim in the session record.
- [Party] The city contains the gates where holy fire consumed the southern human traders, the dwarven commander's location where Agland's letter was delivered, and the party's current skyship boarding point.
- [Party] [Confirmed on 2026-08-29] Fourteen temples stand across the city, and the party enters [Moradin's chamber](./Moradins%20Chamber%20-%20Ola%20Dung%20Lot.md) before boarding the skyship.

## What Dain Knows
- [Party] The southern human traders were seeking their way to Ola Dung Lot.
- [Party] The party has since reached the city by Gondian airship.
- [Party] Agland's sealed letter was delivered to a dwarven commander here.
- [Party] The party is preparing to leave aboard a balloon-like skyship with at least one cannon.

## What The Party Knows
- [Party] Ola Dung Lot is a city in the Samryn Torsten area.
- [Party] Holy fire at the city's gates consumed the southern human traders while leaving their Bag of Holding and Coal Mine Puppet intact. [To verify cause and ward criteria]
- [Party] A dwarven commander here assigned the party to recover captives from cult-raided settlements involving Yuan-ti and to rally humans in the southwest.

## Description
- A mountain city in Samryn Torsten with defended gates, dwarven command facilities and access to large balloon-like skyships.
