## Published Rules - 2014
- 3rd-level conjuration; casting time one action, range 90 feet.
- A 20-foot-radius cloud of yellow gas heavily obscures its area; concentration lasts up to 1 minute.
- A creature completely inside the cloud at the start of its turn makes a Constitution save against poison. Dain's recorded save DC is **16**.
- Failure spends that turn's **action retching and reeling**. The spell does not impose Poisoned; movement, Bonus Actions and reactions remain available, subject to their usual requirements. [Sage Advice](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sac/sage-advice-compendium)
- Creatures immune to poison or needing no breath automatically succeed. It deals no damage and does not choose only enemies; Dain and Harry can be affected unless an exception applies.
- Wet grants fire resistance and cold/lightning vulnerability under Leaky Tent; it provides no poison protection, and this non-damaging cast does not end it.
- Source: [2014 spell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2265-stinking-cloud). Any additional house ruling remains to verify.

## Wind
