## Shared Published Rules
- 3rd-level conjuration; casting time one action, range 90 feet.
- A 20-foot-radius cloud of yellow gas heavily obscures its area; concentration lasts up to 1 minute.
- Constitution saving throws occur at affected creatures' turn starts. Dain's recorded save DC is **16**.
- It deals no damage and does not choose only enemies. Dain and Harry can be affected under the applicable version and exceptions.
- Wet grants fire resistance and cold/lightning vulnerability under Leaky Tent; it provides no poison protection, and this non-damaging cast does not end it.
- Sources: [2014 spell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2265-stinking-cloud), [2024 spell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619091-stinking-cloud). Tom's version and table rulings remain to verify.

## Edition Differences - Table Ruling Pending
- **2014:** a creature completely inside at turn start saves against poison. Failure spends its action retching; creatures immune to poison or needing no breath automatically succeed. Winds of at least 10 mph disperse it after four rounds, or at least 20 mph after one round.
- **2024:** a creature starting its turn in the sphere saves or becomes Poisoned until that turn ends, unable to take an action or Bonus Action while poisoned this way. Strong wind disperses the cloud.
- The camp's forecast does not establish wind strength or exposure inside the tent. No dispersal is recorded.
