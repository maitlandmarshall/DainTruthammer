# Truthammer's Leaky Tent

![Truthammer's Leaky Tent advertisement](../../Assets/Truthammer%20Leaky%20Shelter.png)

## One-Line Summary
A gloriously counterproductive tent spell that appears dry on the outside while water flows through the inside, turning shelter into a precise magical inconvenience.

## Creation Moment
- 2026-05-03: During the forest long rest, Dain held his normal tent in a flowing river and experimented with random somatic components as water ran through it.
- A flash of inspiration revealed the missing element, and he formally created `Truthammer's Leaky Tent`.
- The spell manifested with an explosion audible to the party, immediately dampened into an implosive, swallowed sound.
- A beautiful tent appeared, dry on the outside.
- Dain spent the final hour of the long rest meditating inside the leaky tent, marveling in the creation. [Character-only]

## Confirmed Mechanics
- Cast time: 1 minute.
- Level: 1st-level spell or ritual. [To verify final casting format]
- Duration: 8 hours.
- Area: a 10-foot square tent or hut.
- Mobility: the hut cannot be moved after casting.
- Durability: AC 10; HP 5.
- Core effect: the shelter always leaks water inside, regardless of outside weather.
- Anything inside the tent immediately gains the Wet condition. [To verify condition wording]
- Wet creatures and items are vulnerable to lightning and cold damage.
- Wet creatures and items gain resistance to fire damage.
- The Wet effect lasts until the affected creature or item dries itself, taking 10 minutes, or until it takes any one of fire, cold, or lightning damage.
- [Confirmed table use on 2026-09-12] A **3rd-level casting produces a 30-foot tent**. Exact footprint/height, general size progression and any upcast AC/HP changes remain unspecified. [Session](../../Adventures/2026-09-12.md)

## Design Notes
- This is the named completion of Dain's earlier anti-shelter research.
- The spell is honest in the most Truthhammer way: it promises a leaky shelter and delivers every drop.
- Useful edge cases include fire protection, forced discomfort, campsite sabotage, testing suspicious guests, and absurd environmental preparation.
