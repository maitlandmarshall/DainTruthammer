## Web Timing
- Web does not normally restrain creatures immediately just because the spell appears around them.
- A creature usually makes the Dexterity save when it starts its turn in the webs or enters the webs.
- Under the discussed 2024-style reading, "enters on a turn" can include forced or voluntary entry outside the creature's own turn. Confirm Tom's ruling before relying on this.
- Web remains useful as turn denial, difficult terrain, and area control rather than an instant restraint bomb.

## Restrained Condition
- Speed becomes `0`.
- The creature cannot benefit from bonuses to speed.
- Attack rolls against the creature have advantage.
- The creature's own attack rolls have disadvantage.
- The creature has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.
- For Web, a restrained creature can usually use its action to make a Strength check against Dain's spell save DC, currently `15`, to break free. [To verify exact spell text Tom uses]

## Web And Opportunity Attacks
- Web itself does not trigger opportunity attacks.
- If a creature voluntarily leaves a melee ally's reach while moving through or out of Web, it can provoke as normal.
- Forced movement generally does not provoke opportunity attacks.
- The best tactical use is to place Web so enemies are either stuck near the frontline or must move past allies to reach Dain, Ollyander, or the travelling humans.

## Fire And Web
- If the specific section of Web restraining a creature burns away, that creature is no longer restrained by that Web.
- Burning nearby Web sections may not free the restrained creature if its own section remains.
- Burning Web can trade control for damage and may be risky when the party wants to keep enemies pinned.
